How to play

Offense

  • 1–6Pick a play before the snap
  • SpaceSnap the ball
  • WASDMove (arrows work too)
  • 123Throw to that receiver (or click them)
  • ShiftSprint (uses stamina)
  • SpaceJuke when you have the ball
  • P / FPunt / field goal on 4th down

Defense

  • 123Man / Zone / Blitz
  • Q or TabSwitch to player nearest the ball
  • ClickPick a specific defender
  • SpaceDive tackle (miss = you're on the ground)
  • ShiftSprint

Other

  • CSwitch camera
  • MMute
  • EscPause

Tips: Look at the colored route lines before the snap. When the ball's in the air, steer your receiver to the white ring. Sprint drains the yellow bar under the scoreboard.

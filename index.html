Two-step login (authenticator app)

Two-step login keeps your account safe even if someone gets your password. After your password, the game asks for a 6-digit code that only your phone can make. It’s optional and free.

1. Get an authenticator appOn your phone, download a free authenticator app: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy (App Store or Google Play).
2. Turn it on in the gameGo to Settings, find Two-step login and press Turn on. A QR code shows up.
3. Scan the QR codeIn the app, tap + (or Add account), choose Scan a QR code and point your camera at the code. “Endzone Kings” shows up in the app with your email. Can’t scan? Choose Enter a setup key and type the key shown under the QR code.
4. Type the code to finishThe app shows a 6-digit code for Endzone Kings. Type it in the game and press Confirm. You’ll see “Two-step login is on”.

Logging in with it on

  • Log in with your email or username and password like normal.
  • The game asks for a code. Open the authenticator app and find Endzone Kings with your email under it (like you@example.com), then type the 6 digits.
  • Codes change every 30 seconds. If it says the code is wrong, wait for the next one and type that.
  • Tick Remember this device for 10 days on your own phone or computer and you won’t need a code there for 10 days. Don’t tick it on a shared or school device.

If something goes wrong

  • New phone: before you switch, turn two-step login off in Settings (it asks for a code), then turn it back on with the new phone. Google and Microsoft Authenticator can also move your codes to a new phone.
  • Lost your phone: you won’t be able to make codes. Use the Feedback button on the main menu (choose Need help) and the dev team can reset it for you.
  • Turning it off: Settings, Two-step login, Turn off, then type a code from the app.

What is Endzone Kings?

Endzone Kings is a 3D arcade football game you play right in your browser, made by Habir Studios. There's nothing to download or install, and nothing to pay for. It's built to feel like a big primetime broadcast: a packed stadium under the lights, a crowd that goes wild on big plays, a play-by-play announcer, and a TV-style scoreboard.

The league

There are 32 teams, each named after its city, and every roster is full of made-up players with real ratings. Speed, hands, arm strength, accuracy, elusiveness, blocking, pass rush, tackling and coverage all change how a player moves and what he can do. A fast receiver really does run past slow corners, and an accurate quarterback really does hit the window.

Calling plays

Every team has its own offensive and defensive playbook built around a coaching style, like an air raid passing attack, a ground-and-pound run game, a West Coast short-passing scheme, a blitz-heavy defense, or a zone defense that takes away the deep ball. Each team also has a signature play you won't find in anyone else's book. There are 45 offensive plays across eight formations (shotgun, trips, bunch, spread, empty, pistol, I-form and singleback) and 20 defenses. Offense is split into quick passes, deep shots, runs, play action, screens and special teams. Defense is split into man coverage, zone coverage and blitzes.

On the field

You control the action on every snap. On offense, throw to your receivers with 1, 2 or 3 (or just click them), then juke, spin, stiff-arm and hurdle your way upfield after the catch. On defense, switch to any player, jump the route and play the ball for the interception, or dive at the ball carrier. You can kick field goals, punt, run fakes, kick the extra point or go for two. If it's tied at the end of regulation, overtime is next score wins.

Ways to play

  • Play Now: pick any two teams and a difficulty and take on the CPU.
  • Season: take one team through a 17-game, 18-week schedule with live division standings, a 14-team playoff (division winners plus three wild cards per conference) and the Kings Bowl.
  • Ultimate Team: open packs of player cards, build your own squad, train your players and take them into games, online too.
  • Online H2H: host a game in the public lobby or send a friend a code. Chat with T.
  • Play Creator: draw your own offense, defense and special teams plays and call them in any game.
  • Practice & Drills: practice offense or defense with no clock, or see how many points you can put up in the two-minute drill and chase the leaderboard.
  • Rosters: look up every player's ratings on all 32 teams.

It plays with a keyboard and mouse on a computer, and with an on-screen joystick and buttons on a phone or tablet.

Game day

Every game can be played in clear skies, rain, snow or fog, during the day, at dusk or under the lights. Rain makes the ball slippery and snow slows everyone down. At the half you get a halftime show with team stats and each side's leaders. Referees are on the field and throw flags for holding, pass interference, false starts, offsides, roughing the passer and facemasks. Big plays swing the momentum meter, and the team that owns it plays a step faster. Between plays you can watch an instant replay, and every game ends with a Player of the Game and the night's highlights.

At the line

After you pick a pass play, press H to change any receiver's route on the spot with eight options each: go, slant, out, curl, post, corner, drag or comeback for receivers, and wheel, flat, angle, swing, seam, Texas, check down or block for the back. Pick your emote in the Locker Room and hold Tab after any play to hit it.

The grind

Every game you play pays coins and XP. Level up for coin bonuses and free packs, climb ranked divisions from Bronze to Kings, knock out daily and weekly challenges for big rewards, and unlock 24 achievements. Coins only come from playing. Nothing in Endzone Kings costs real money.

Your account

Endzone Kings has its own sign-up and log-in, so you don't need a Google account. Guests can jump into Quick Play against the CPU. A free account unlocks everything else: Ultimate Team, coins, levels, ranked, challenges, Season, online games and the Play Creator. Your progress follows you to any device.

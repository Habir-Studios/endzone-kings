Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Endzone Kings is a free browser football game made by Habir Studios. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your Google account email and basic profile (name and picture).
  • Game data: your username, level, rank, coins, records, game history and settings.
  • Security data: two-step verification status if you turn it on.

How we use it

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving progress, leaderboards, online matches and account security. We don't sell your data or show ads based on it.

Where it's stored

Account and game data is stored with Supabase. Some settings are stored in your browser.

Who can see it

Other players can see your username, level, rank and records. Your email is private; only the game owner can look it up for support and moderation.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. That removes your account and game data.

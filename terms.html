1. Agreeing to these terms

These terms are an agreement between you and Habir Studios ("we", "us", "our"). They cover Endzone Kings wherever you play it (our website, itch.io or a saved home-screen app) and our studio website. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and is part of these terms. If you don't agree, please don't play.

2. Who can play

  • Endzone Kings isn't made for children under 13. You need to be at least 13 to make an account.
  • If you're under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian, and they should read these terms with you.
  • You can't play if we've banned you before, unless we've told you that you can come back.

3. Your account

  • One person per account. Don't share, sell, buy, trade or give away accounts.
  • Give a real email address you can get to. It's how you log in, reset your password and get account emails.
  • Keep your password and authenticator codes secret. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • If you think someone else got into your account, change your password, sign out your other devices in Settings, and email us.
  • Each email can only be used by one account.
  • Guests can play Quick Play without an account. Guest progress lives only in that browser and can be lost if browser data is cleared.

4. Usernames

  • Usernames are 3 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores, and each one can only belong to one player.
  • No offensive, hateful, sexual or threatening names, and no names that pretend to be someone else (another player, a staff member, Habir Studios, a real athlete or a real company).
  • You can change your username in Settings once every 14 days.
  • We can change or remove a username that breaks these rules, and we can take back a name from an account that's been banned or deleted.

5. Playing fair

Endzone Kings is only fun if everyone plays by the same rules. You agree not to:

  • Use hacks, cheats, mods, scripts, bots, macros, speed hacks or any tool that changes how the game runs or gives you an unfair advantage.
  • Edit your save, your browser storage or the game's code to change coins, cards, ratings, scores, records or anything else.
  • Exploit bugs or glitches on purpose. If you find one, report it with the Feedback button instead.
  • Boost or fix games: arranging results, letting someone win, or using a second account to farm wins, coins or rank.
  • Try to get into other players' accounts or data, our database, or parts of the game you aren't supposed to reach, or try to overload or break our servers.

The game has a built-in anti-cheat that catches things that can't happen in normal play. Skill can't trigger it, and neither can lag or a slow connection. See section 12 for what happens when it does.

6. Being respectful

In chat, messages, names, bios, clips, trade notes and anywhere else in the game, don't:

  • Harass, bully, threaten or stalk anyone
  • Post hate speech or attack people for who they are
  • Post sexual content, or anything involving minors in any inappropriate way
  • Share someone's private information (their real name, address, school, phone number, photos and so on)
  • Spam, scam, or post links meant to trick people or steal accounts
  • Pretend to be staff, Habir Studios or another player
  • Encourage anyone to hurt themselves or others

If someone breaks these rules, use the Report button on their profile. You can also stop following them and stop messaging them.

7. Online play

  • Online games need a working internet connection. Bad connections can cause lag or disconnects, and we can't promise every online game will finish.
  • Leaving a game that counts (Online H2H, Ultimate Team, Season or Franchise) before it ends counts as a loss. So does getting removed for being idle too long.
  • If your opponent quits, you get the win.
  • Signed-in players' live games can be watched by friends from their profile. Spectators can't see play calls.
  • Online Franchise leagues are run by the players in them. The league owner can start the league, and we can step in if a league is being used to break these rules.

8. Coins, cards and items

  • Coins, Training Points, packs, player cards, celebrations, ranks, levels and everything else you earn in the game are virtual items. They have no real-world value and can't be exchanged for real money.
  • Nothing in Endzone Kings costs real money. Don't buy or sell accounts, coins or items for real money or anything outside the game. If you do, we can remove the items and the accounts involved.
  • You have permission to use your items in the game, but you don't own them. We can change, rebalance, rename or remove items, prices, pack odds and rewards to keep the game balanced.
  • If a bug or glitch gives you items you shouldn't have, we can take them back.
  • Pack contents are random. The odds can change between updates.

9. Trading

  • You can trade players, packs, coins and Training Points with other players. Starters in your lineup can't be traded until you take them out.
  • Once a trade is accepted, it's final. We don't undo trades you regret.
  • Only trade with people you trust. Don't use trades to scam people, to move items between your own accounts, or as part of any real-money deal.
  • We can cancel trades that break these rules and remove what was traded.

10. Seasons and resets

Endzone Kings runs in seasons. When a new season starts, we may reset progress so everyone starts equal: levels, coins, Ultimate Team, records, stats, leagues and other progress can go back to the start. Your account, username and password stay. We'll announce resets in the patch notes. Resets aren't a reason for refunds or compensation (there's nothing to refund anyway, since the game is free).

11. Your content

  • Your content is the stuff you create: your bio, messages, clips, custom plays, trade notes, league names and feedback.
  • You keep whatever rights you have in it. By posting it in the game, you give Habir Studios permission to store it, show it in the game, and use it to run and improve the game. For example, we can show your clips to other players or use your bug report to fix the game.
  • Only post things you're allowed to post. Don't post other people's copyrighted work.
  • We can remove any content that breaks these rules, and we aren't responsible for what other players post.

12. Reports, flags and bans

If you break these terms, we can do any of these, depending on how serious it is:

  • Warn you
  • Remove content, rename your account, or take back items, coins or rewards
  • Reset your progress, records or rank
  • Temporarily suspend you or permanently ban you

The anti-cheat can flag an account automatically when a game breaks the game's own rules, and an account that gets flagged repeatedly is banned automatically. Staff review player reports and can ban accounts by hand. If you think we made a mistake, email us from the email on your account and explain what happened. We'll look into it.

We can change, pause or stop any part of the game, or the whole game, at any time. We'll try to give a heads-up in the patch notes for big changes.
